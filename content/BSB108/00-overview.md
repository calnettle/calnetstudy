# BSB108 — Unit Overview

QUT · 1st Year · Summer · **BSB108 Business Environment**

The unit teaches you to scan a national business environment you know
nothing about, find credible data on it fast, and write it up for a client
who knows even less. The vehicle for the whole semester is one focus
sector: **Australian butter and dairy product manufacturing** (ANZSIC
1133 / IBISWorld C1133c).

## The four modules

The unit is built in four modules, and each module runs the same weekly
loop:

| Activity | Length | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Lecture | 50 min | Broad introduction; "signposts" the week's content and shows how it fits together |
| Drilling Down | 90 min | Independent guided reading and skills practice. No answers are published — it is preparation, not a worksheet |
| Tutorial | 110 min | Talking Heads → tutorial activities → PEST/A2 Prep → Q&A forum |
| Review quiz | short | **Formative only.** Not part of the assessment |
| Draft the assessment element | — | Each module ends with one self-contained section of A1 or A2 to draft |

The design intent is that if you draft the flagged element every week, you
have a complete report a week before submission.

> **Drilling Down is not optional prep you can skip.** Tutorial 3 runs
> directly off Drilling Down 3's Tables 1–3; Tutorial 7 runs off Drilling
> Down 7's freedom-camping and business-freedom tasks. If you have not done
> the reading, the tutorial is 110 minutes of watching other people work.

## Assessment

| Assessment | Weight | Mode | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 — Insight report: national business environment | 50% | Individual | 2,500 words max | Word or PDF |
| A2 — Insight report: regulatory and international business environment | 50% | Individual | 2,500 words max | Word or PDF |

Both are written for the same fictional client: an anonymous investor who
has asked for an overview of the Australian business environment in which
the focus sector operates. You are told nothing else about them.

**Word budgets the unit recommends:**

```
A1 (2,500 words)                     A2 (2,500 words)
  abstract              ~200           abstract              ~200
  definition of sector  ~200           macroeconomic policy  ~575
  Political             ~525           policy & regulation   ~575
  Economic              ~525           international trade   ~575
  Social                ~525           geopolitical          ~575
  Technology            ~525           ------------------------
  ------------------------             total               2,500
  total               2,500
```

The word count **includes** in-text citations, figures and tables. It
**excludes** the reference list.

## Formatting rules (both reports)

```
margins        2 cm top, bottom and both sides
body font      Times Roman, Arial or Calibri, 11 pt min / 12 pt max
line spacing   Multiple 1.15
headings       same fonts, 14 pt min / 18 pt max, bold
page numbers   bottom right, 9 pt
sections       each new section starts on a new page
referencing    APA
```

> **Screen-grabbing a figure is an automatic fail against criterion PC3.1.**
> The task sheet treats a pasted screenshot — including from the ABS, RBA
> or OECD data interfaces — as a potential copyright breach, because the
> simulated deliverable goes to a third-party client and out of your
> control. You must download the data and build the figure yourself in
> Excel. If you cannot get the underlying data, describe the published
> figure in words and cite it instead.

## Generative AI rules differ between A1 and A2

| | A1 | A2 |
|---|---|---|
| Use GenAI to write the analysis | **No** | **No** |
| Use GenAI to choose sources or generate data/figures | **No** | **No** |
| Use GenAI to check grammar, syntax and clarity of your own draft | Yes | Yes |
| Use GenAI to help you study and explore the topic | Not stated as permitted | **Yes** |
| Copy or paraphrase AI-generated content into the submission | **No** | **No** |

Both reports are checked by Turnitin; A1 is checked for AI as well as
originality. You may be asked to demonstrate authorship, so keep drafts.

> **The difference is narrow but real.** A1 permits GenAI only as a
> proofreader of text you already wrote. A2 additionally permits it as a
> study aid. Neither permits AI-generated prose, AI-selected sources, or
> AI-generated figures in the submitted file. The unit's own Week 4
> materials make the point empirically: the tutor's document shows seven
> Copilot attempts at an ABS GDP chart and notes that **some of them
> contain simulated data**, not ABS data at all.

```
                9 Jan 2025 = Thursday      9 Jan 2026 = Friday   ✓
```

and the year was not updated. Treat the due dates as 9 January and

## How these notes are built

Concept → formula → worked example with real numbers → the trap that costs
marks. Blockquotes are amber callouts and are reserved for something that
changes an answer. Checkpoint questions sit in tap-to-reveal blocks.

Every number was recomputed before it was written down. That process found
errors in the unit's own model answers and in the IBISWorld report — they
are flagged inline with the arithmetic, not silently corrected. Where the
source is genuinely ambiguous, both readings are given and the
conventional one is named.
