# BSB106 — Unit Overview

QUT · 1st Year, Semester 1 · **Dynamic Markets**

A frameworks unit, not a numbers unit. Almost every mark comes from picking
the right framework, applying it to a specific organisation with evidence,
and saying what the analysis *means*. The arithmetic that does appear —
market shares, growth rates, relative market share for the BCG matrix — is
easy to get wrong in ways markers notice.

## Assessment

Weights and dates below come from the Assignment 1 and Assignment 2 briefs
and the Tutorial 10 deck in the source folder. **They are Semester 1 2025
dates — treat them as the shape of the unit, not as current deadlines.**

| Assessment | Task | Weight | Individual / Group |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Market Landscape Analysis — team contract, BEC, 750-word summary | 20% | Group (~5) |
| 2 | Business Model Analysis — revised contract, BMC, 5-minute oral presentation | 30% | Group (same team) |
| 3 | Disruption Analysis Report (2,000 words) + Critical Reflection (500 words) | 50% | Individual |

20 + 30 + 50 = 100%. Each of A1 and A2 also carries a **peer evaluation**
component scored 1–7 by teammates.

The three assessments are a chain, not three separate tasks:

```
A1  analyse the market landscape of the parent organisation   → BEC
A2  analyse the business model of one of its brands           → BMC
A3  hit that business model with a disruption and respond     → revised BEC + BMC
```

Assignment 3's brief is explicit that A1 and A2 content feeds A3, and the
Tutorial 10 deck repeats it: *"Content from Assignments 1 and 2 will inform
Assignment 3 so keep that fresh in mind."* A weak BEC in Week 6 is still
costing you marks in Week 13.

## Hard formatting rules that cost marks

These are stated in the briefs, and they are not soft:

- **BEC: 1 slide, maximum 60 words per quadrant**, legible printed A4
  landscape. No references on the canvas.
- **BMC: 1 slide, maximum 60 words per segment**, same conditions.
- **A1 summary: 750 words**, provided template, **Times 12pt, 1.5 line
  spacing, 2.54cm margins**, "do not edit template formatting". Use the
  template's headings.
- **A2 deck: 10 slides maximum** including the BMC slide, deliverable in
  **5 minutes**, plus up to 5 minutes of questions.
- **A3: 2,000-word report and 500-word reflection.**
- Word limits carry a **±10% margin of error**; markers "will otherwise not
  consider content beyond stated limits". Content past the limit is not
  penalised, it is *ignored* — so anything past the cut is wasted effort.
- Third person, professional register. The stated audience for A1 and A2 is
  **the senior management team, including the CEO, of the case organisation**
  — not your tutor.
- APA referencing in the written documents. Generative AI may be used to
  learn about topics but must not be copied in, and must not be cited.

> **The 60-word limit is per quadrant, and it bites.** Team A's submitted A1
> canvas ran 59 / 63 / 69 / 76 words across the four quadrants. Even with the
> ±10% margin (66 words), Industry forces and Market forces were over. Count
> the words in each box before you submit — a canvas is the one artefact
> where a marker can check the limit in five seconds.

## Case organisation

The parent organisation and its brands are set on Canvas each semester. In
Semester 1 2025 it was **Procter & Gamble**, with the nominated Australian
brands **Gillette** (male toiletries), **Oral-B** (oral hygiene), **Olay**
(skincare) and **Pantene** (haircare). Four MarketLine industry profiles for
those markets were supplied as the preliminary business reports.

Every worked example in these notes uses that case, because that is what the
source material contains. The frameworks transfer unchanged to a different
organisation.

## What is and isn't in the source folder

Worth knowing, because it shapes what these notes can say:

**Present** — the Assignment 1 and Assignment 2 briefs, the Tutorial 10
deck (Disruption: Impacts), four MarketLine Australia industry profiles, two
academic readings (Ramya & Mohamed Ali on consumer buying behaviour; Bara,
Dela Cruz & Mendoza on sustainable packaging), one methods reading (Meijer et

full A1, A2 and A3 submissions.

**Absent** — there are **no lecture slide decks and no tutorial materials
other than Tutorial 10**. There is also **no Assignment 3 brief**, so the six
hypothetical disruptions it lists are unknown apart from the one Cal chose
(severe weather damaging Australian ports). Where these notes state something
about lecture content, it is inferred from what the assignments and the
tutorial actually assess, and is labelled as such. Two files in `A2/` —
*Orange and Black Minimalist Creative Brief Presentation* and its 21 exported
JPEGs — are an unused Canva template still full of Lorem ipsum; there is no
BSB106 content in them.

## How these notes are built

Concept → framework → worked example with real figures from the source →
the trap that costs marks. Checkpoint questions in tap-to-reveal blocks.

Every figure quoted from the MarketLine profiles was recomputed before it was
written down: segment values reconciled to stated totals, share tables summed
to 100%, and every CAGR rebuilt from the endpoints. Where Team A's submitted
work disagrees with the supplied source — and it does, in several places —
the note shows the arithmetic, says which reading is right, and tells you to
confirm with your tutor rather than quietly picking a side.
