| **Statement classification** | Which statement each item belongs on — [Tutorial 2](#/AYB250/15-tutorial-2-solutions) Q1 is exactly this |
| **The four planning ratios** | All four from memory, with the direction of "good" |

```
Net worth ratio     = Net worth / Total assets × 100          higher better
Liquidity ratio     = Liquid assets / Current debt × 100      higher better
Savings ratio       = Savings / Net income × 100              higher better
Debt service ratio  = (Annual debt commitments / 12)
                      ÷ (Annual net income / 12) × 100        LOWER better
```

> **The definitions decide the answer, not the formulas.** *Current debt*
> is the **next twelve months of repayments** — a flow, not a balance.
> *Savings* includes investments and extra super, not just the surplus.
> *Net income* means after tax. Trap 1 in the unit's list is precisely
> this: **flow versus stock**.

## The 36 Traps — use them as a checklist

[Note 24](#/AYB250/24-formula-sheet-and-exam-traps) ranks all 36 by how
much they cost. **Read it first, not last.** If you can state what each
one is about from its first five words, you know the unit.

The six that cost the most, in order: flow versus stock · cutting gross
income without recomputing tax · mixing periodic and effective rates ·
applying a marginal rate to the whole income · nominal ranking ≠
effective ranking · adding future values struck at different dates.
