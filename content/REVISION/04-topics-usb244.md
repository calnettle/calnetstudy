## The Calculation Set — all of it

Five things. If you can do these five, USB244's arithmetic is finished.

```
1.  CV = NI / CR          and both rearrangements, plus 1/CR per $1
2.  WALE (income) = Σ(income × term) / Σ(income)
    WALE (area)   = Σ(area   × term) / Σ(area)
    remaining term = (expiry − EOI) / 365.25
3.  Effective rent from face rent and an incentive
4.  Outgoings recovery → net income → value
5.  GOCR = gross occupancy cost / MAT      (anchors 2–4%, specialty 8–15%)
    Productivity = MAT / lettable area
```

Portfolio WALE is income- or area-weighted across assets — **never** the
simple average of asset WALEs.

## Traps — the recurring five

1. **Which area measure** — NLA, GLA or GLAR.
2. **Face versus effective rent** — incentives make face rents
   incomparable.
3. **Land tax is recoverable — except under a Queensland retail shop
   lease.**
4. **Capital, financing or promotional costs are not outgoings.**
   Servicing the lift is; replacing it is not.
5. **A low recovery rate is not automatically bad management** — check
   what share of GLA sits with a zero-recovery anchor first.
