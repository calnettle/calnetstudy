## How a Block Works

```
Block 1   5 min   closed-book recall of yesterday's outputs (no notes)
          45 min  the material named below
          10 min  the OUTPUT, written closed book
Block 2   50 min  material  +  10 min output
Block 3   50 min  material  +  10 min output
```

1. **Do the blocks in the order given.** Block 1 is always the hardest
   new material of the day, so it goes first while you are fresh.
2. **Every block ends with its output, written closed book.** That line
   is the point of the hour. If the hour runs out, cut the reading and
   still write the output.
3. **When a block overruns, stop at 60 minutes anyway.** Note what you
   didn't finish and pick it up in the next sweep (18 Oct, 25 Oct,
   1 Nov). Don't let one block take the next one's time.
4. **When a whole day is lost, don't push the plan back.** Skip it. At
   the next sweep, do the most important block you missed instead of
   the sweep, choosing EFB335 or AYB250 over the property units.
5. **If you already worked a block's material in the first plan,** do
   its self-test or tutorial first. If you get most of it, spend the
   rest of the hour on the output and the traps, then stop.

> **Where the files are.** Links go to the site. Blocks marked
> **📁 deck** use the lecturer's own files, which are not on the site,
> in `QUT › 2nd Year › Sem 2 › <unit>`. Slide numbers are given so you
> know where to stop.

> **EFB335 Topics 5–10 have no notes on the site yet.** You learn them

## If the Plan Slips

| What happened | Do this |
|---|---|
| Lost one day | Skip it. At the next sweep, do its EFB335 or AYB250 block instead of the sweep |
| Lost two or more days in Weeks 1–2 | Drop the USB244 and USB245 blocks in Weeks 1–3, which are only there to keep those units warm, and use them for the missed EFB335/AYB250 blocks. The property units' late blocks stay |
| A deck block takes two hours | Finish it in the next block and push that block's unit to the next sweep. Topics 5–10 are worth the time, since they carry 36 of the sample's 40 marks |
| You feel solid on a unit early | Don't drop its blocks. Turn them into closed-book practice from that unit's question bank |
| New material lands (AYB250 plan construction, USB244 Week 12, USB245 Week 12) | Add it to that week's sweep, then ask Trent to write it up |
