### EFB335 · Topic 6 (1 of 2): passive and active

*Learned Sun 1 Nov · [open the lesson](#/EFB335/24-plain-english-topic-6-equity-strategies)*

**Questions**

1. Pre-tax return 9%, tax-adjusted return 7.5%. Tax cost ratio?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

1 − 1.075 ÷ 1.09 = **1.38%**.

</details>

2. Full replication vs sampling: the trade-off?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Replication gives the **lowest tracking error** but the **highest costs**. Sampling is **cheaper** but tracks less closely.

</details>

**Revision notes**

- **Passive:** track an index; judged on **tracking error** = SD of (Rp − Rb). **Active:** beat the benchmark risk-adjusted, net of costs (alpha).
- Index construction: **full replication** (closest, costliest), **sampling**, **optimisation**.
- **ETF** trades on an exchange (can be shorted); an index fund is bought from the manager.
- Active styles: top-down vs bottom-up, **130/30**, contrarian vs momentum, factor-based.
- **Tax cost ratio** = 1 − (1 + TAR) ÷ (1 + PTR). High turnover → higher TCR (active funds).

**Strategic:** long-term policy weights from long-run estimates, rebalanced periodically. **Tactical:** frequent short-term shifts to exploit perceived mispricing (mean reversion).

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It **reduces the share weighting and moves into T-bills** to protect a floor value.

</details>

- **Style analysis** matches a manager's returns to style indexes (size × value/growth grid).
- Asset allocation: **integrated** (markets + investor), **strategic** (long-run policy, rebalance), **tactical** (short-term, contrarian), **insured** (two assets, protect a floor).
- Super is ~**85%** of Australian managed funds: compulsory SG, preservation, tax concessions, ageing population.

### AYB250 · Topic 5b: leveraged investing and margin lending

*Learned Sun 1 Nov · [open the lesson](#/AYB250/09-topic-5-leveraged-investing)*

**Questions**

1. Debt $200,000, your capital $400,000. Gearing ratio and LVR?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Gearing = 200 ÷ 400 = **0.5**. LVR = 200 ÷ 600 = **33%**.

</details>

2. $40,000 equity plus a $60,000 margin loan. The market falls 25%. New LVR?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Portfolio = $75,000; LVR = 60 ÷ 75 = **80%** (was 60%).

</details>

**Revision notes**

- Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.
- **Gearing ratio = debt ÷ your capital. LVR = debt ÷ total value.**
- Positive gearing: income > costs (taxable). Negative: costs > income (loss offsets other income, subject to the 2026 changes for residential).
- Margin loans: max LVR per security plus a **5–10% buffer**; a margin call must be met in **24 hours** (cash, more security, or sell).

### USB245 · Topic 5: NPV, IRR and return measures

*Learned Sun 1 Nov · [open the lesson](#/USB245/05-topic-5-return-measurements)*

**Questions**

1. Invest $100 now, receive $60 at the end of years 1 and 2, required return 10%. NPV?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

54.55 + 49.59 − 100 = **+$4.13** → accept.

</details>

2. NPV and IRR rank two projects differently. Which do you follow?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**NPV**.

</details>

**Revision notes**

- Static measures: single-year ratios, ignore time value and later years.
- **NPV = Σ CFₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ − CF₀.** Positive = accept.
- **IRR** = the rate making NPV zero; accept if IRR > required return.
- Rankings can conflict (**scale, timing, horizon**); non-conventional cashflows give multiple IRRs. **Decide by NPV.**
- Required return: fix the rate, solve the price. Expected return: fix the price, solve the rate.

### USB244 · Topic 5: taxation and decision-making

*Learned Sun 1 Nov · [open the lesson](#/USB244/05-topic-5-taxation-and-decision-making)*

**Questions**

1. Opex vs capex, one example each and the tax treatment?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Opex** (e.g. repairing part of a roof) deductible **immediately**. **Capex** (e.g. replacing it with a better material) deducted **over time**.

</details>

2. GST on residential rent, commercial rent, and a going-concern sale?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Residential: **no GST, no credits**. Commercial: **GST with input credits**. Going concern: **GST-free**.

</details>

**Revision notes**

- Tax stack: federal (income tax, CGT, GST), state (land tax, stamp duty), council (rates).
- **OPEX** (repair, restore) 100% deductible now; **CAPEX** (improve, add value) over time; buildings **2.5% over 40 years**.
- **GST:** residential rent none; commercial fully in; going-concern sales GST-free.
- Negative gearing (tax loss) ≠ negative cash flow; depreciation is the non-cash wedge.
- Decision tools: **CBA, feasibility, LCC, value management, scenario planning**.
