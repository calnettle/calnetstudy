# USB144 — Unit Overview

QUT · 1st Year, Semester 2 · **Investment Valuation**

The unit that teaches you to put a number on an income-producing property.
Everything funnels into one equation — `Value = NOI / Cap rate` — and the
whole semester is spent learning how to defend both halves of it.

## Unit Map

| # | Note | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Topic 1 — Foundations of Investment Valuation | Motives to acquire, NOI / cap rate / yield / ROI, purpose of a valuation, the profession and its regulation, office / retail / industrial sectors, property as an asset class, the six drivers of asset value |
| 02 | Topic 2 — Time Value of Money | Simple and compound interest, compounding frequency, PV and FV of a lump sum and of an annuity, sinking funds, Excel and the financial calculator |
| 03 | Topic 3 — Commercial Leases, Rent Reviews and Market Evidence | Lease structure and synopsis, passing vs market rent, the five review types, the five-step market rent review, analysing lease and sales evidence, the four yields, WALE |
| 04 | Topic 4 — The Capitalisation Method | The NOI build-up, gross vs net rent, vacancy, outgoings, the cap rate, Year's Purchase, incentives, below-the-line adjustments, over- and under-rented property |
| 05 | Topic 5 — DCF and Hypothetical Development | The six DCF elements, terminal value, discount rate, NPV, IRR, the residual/hypothetical development method |
| 06 | Tutorial Worked Solutions | Every tutorial exercise worked end to end, including the ones the unit never released answers for |
| 07 | Extra Practice Questions | Exam-style questions across all five topics |
| 08 | Formula Sheet & Exam Traps | Everything on one page |

## Learning outcomes

Straight from the Week 1 deck:

1. **Analyse property markets.**
2. **Apply valuation methodologies** — the deck adds its own emphasis here in
   capitals: *understand and apply the capitalisation method*.
3. **Interpret, critically analyse and synthesise data.**
4. **Communicate with a range of stakeholders** including clients,
   consultants, lenders, investors and developers.

Outcome 2 is the exam. Outcomes 3 and 4 are the two reports.

## What is and is not in the source folder

The lecture folder holds **Weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 11 only**. Weeks 6–10 and
12–13 are not there, so these notes cover five topic blocks, not a full
thirteen-week unit. If those decks turn up, the gaps are: whatever sits
between the capitalisation method and Week 11's "other valuation approaches".

Tutorials in the folder: T01 drivers of asset value, T03 time value of money,
T04 capitalisation method, T05 WALE calculation (plus its Excel workbook), and
T06 mid-semester exam preparation.

The `A2 Resources` folder is the evidence pack for the two reports — the
subject property is **459 Tufnell Road, Banyo** (Lots 1 and 2 on SP219017, two
adjoining industrial lots leased to QUT as its Pilot Plant research facility).
It contains two current title searches, the survey plan, two Brisbane City
Council FloodWise reports, and five Cushman & Wakefield lease-analysis
brochures used as comparable lease evidence. Those brochures are the cleanest
worked example of "analyse the lease evidence to a rate per m² of GLA" you
will find, and Topic 3 uses them.

## How these notes are built

Concept → formula → worked example with real numbers → the trap that costs
marks, with a checkpoint quiz in a tap-to-reveal block at the end of each
topic.

Every number in these notes was recomputed in Python before it was written
down, and again after editing. The unit's own material contains several
arithmetic and labelling errors; each one is shown with the working rather
than quietly corrected. The running list is at the end of the formula sheet.
