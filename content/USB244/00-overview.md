## Learning outcomes

Straight from the unit outline — assessment is written against these, so
phrase your report conclusions in their language:

1. **Measure, benchmark and maximise** the performance of real property
   assets by applying economic, managerial, legal, physical and technical
   aspects of property and asset management.
2. **Analyse the role of property managers** in asset management, and
   evaluate their impact on the performance of real property assets in a
   diversified investment portfolio.
3. **Present solutions** through written communication.
4. **Collaborate strategically** in professional contexts to develop the
   strategic asset management (SAM) plan.

## Assessment

| Assessment | Due | Weight | Individual / Group |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (Part A) — Report, historical analysis | 2 Sep 2026, 11:59pm | 20% | Individual |
| 1 (Part B) — Report, forecasting and strategy | 7 Oct 2026, 11:59pm | 30% | Group of 4 |
| 2 — Final exam | QUT Central Examination | 50% | Individual |

**Part A feeds Part B.** Part A reviews **one** property — commercial,
retail or industrial, in South East Queensland, 3+ tenants, not Richlands
Plaza or 41 Park Road Milton — from its Information Memorandum, looking back
three years from the **EOI date**. Site visits are optional enrichment, not
an assessment requirement. Each group member uses a different property, and
at least three of those become the group's Part B portfolio.

The chain is: source an eligible IM → review it individually → pool at least
three properties into a group forecast, SAM plan and scenario analysis.

Full guides: [Part A](#/USB244/10-assessment-1-part-a-guide) ·
[Part B](#/USB244/11-assessment-1-part-b-sam-plan).

## How these notes are built

Same structure as EFB335: concept → formula → worked example with real
numbers → the trap that costs marks, with a checkpoint quiz in a
tap-to-reveal block at the end of each major section.

Every number in these notes was recomputed and reconciled before it was
written down. Where the source material contradicts itself — and it does,
in a few places — the note shows the arithmetic and says which reading is
right rather than quietly picking one.
